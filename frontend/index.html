New here? What do these order choices mean?

Buy opens or adds to a position. Sell closes shares you own first; selling more may open a short position.

Market aims to trade now. Limit waits for your chosen price and may never fill. Stop-limit activates a limit order at a trigger, but a price gap can leave it unfilled.

The ask is the price used for an immediate buy and the bid for an immediate sell in this simulator. Their difference is the spread. An option order uses whole lots, which contain many units.
