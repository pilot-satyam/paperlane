# Stock-specific option chains

`options.json` contains 87,506 NSE CE/PE contract metadata rows from the Groww CSV retrieved on 2026-10-26: https://growwapi-assets.groww.in/instruments/instrument.csv. Source SHA-256: `f34513908b688b2fbb3a878d2660ef6cd7bca271320c9cbd5f076e7577baa2e4`. Documentation: https://groww.in/trade-api/docs/curl/instruments. The compact column/row encoding preserves each contract's token, trading symbol, underlying symbol/token, expiry, strike, option type, lot, tick, dealing flags and reserved status. This dated broker snapshot is not a guarantee of present exchange availability. Futures and BSE option contracts are not imported.

Rebuild using `python3 scripts/import_options.py /path/to/instrument.csv --retrieved-at YYYY-MM-DD`. Duplicate exchange/FNO/token IDs fail the import.

`GET /api/options?symbol=<active-symbol-or-cash-catalogue-id>&expiry=YYYY-MM-DD` reads the selected underlying's chain without activating contracts. Expiry is optional and defaults to the earliest unexpired expiry. Selected active options resolve back to their underlying. BSE cash maps to an unambiguous NSE EQ instrument by ISIN; it never substitutes an unrelated index. A stock without a matching unexpired NSE chain returns an empty chain and explanation. Legacy NIFTY selection opens the imported NIFTY chain. Premiums and underlying values are synthetic; a BSE quote and canonical NSE synthetic quote may differ.

Response: `{mode, underlying:{symbol,display_symbol,ltp,trading_symbol,catalogue_id}, expiries, expiry, contracts:[{id,symbol,trading_symbol,underlying_symbol,underlying_token,option_type,strike,expiry,lot_size,tick_size,buy_allowed,sell_allowed,is_reserved,ltp,bid,ask,can_trade,restriction}], notes, coverage}`. `notes` is a string. Contracts sort by strike and type. All current-expiry strikes are returned for frontend filtering.

`POST /api/options/activate {id}` activates only the chosen contract and, when needed, its canonical NSE underlying. Returns `{symbol,instrument}`; `instrument.contract_id` identifies snapshot contracts. The existing 100-active-instrument cap covers both, checked before activation. Existing cash quotes, history, positions and trades are preserved. A newly activated underlying starts at the median unreserved strike of its nearest expiry, solely to give the synthetic practice chain a useful baseline. This is not a valuation or real price.

Premiums are an illustrative intrinsic-value calculation plus `underlying × 0.01 × sqrt(days / 30)` time value, rounded to the snapshot tick. They are not a calibrated option pricing model, and there are no live quotes, Greeks, IV, OI, volume, liquidity or queue simulation. Option short-margin estimates use 30% of the option's actual synthetic underlying value × units, not NIFTY for every stock. This remains an illustrative estimate, not exchange SPAN margin.

The simulator conservatively disables reserved contracts for activation. Contract lot/tick and provider buy/sell flags govern orders. At 15:30 India time on the recorded expiry date, activation and all new orders are disabled; pending orders cancel on the next tick, releasing reservations. Quotes for expired contracts freeze. Existing expired positions are retained visibly for audit and **are not settled**; physical delivery, exercise, assignment, expiry holidays, and settlement are not implemented. The dated snapshot eventually has no unexpired contracts until deliberately refreshed. Legacy demo options retain their original identity/history.

Charts can retain up to 1,800 synthetic quote points per active instrument, accumulated while the server runs; this is not historical market data.
